TTD Govinda Autofill (Free) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

TTD Govinda Autofill (Free) is a free Chrome extension, offered as a seva, that saves pilgrim details on your own computer and fills them into Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) booking forms when you ask it to. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TTD.

What the extension stores

Only what you type into it:

  • Pilgrim details: name, gender, date of birth or age, ID proof type and number (such as Aadhaar or passport), and passport, visa or OCI details.
  • Optional Srivari Seva details: mobile, email, address, blood group, gothram, education, job, languages, reference contacts, and a photo or document you choose to attach.
  • Contact details for the booking, your language and reminder settings, and your quota release schedule.

Where it is stored

Everything is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server. The extension has no backend, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not load code from the internet.

If you set a master password, your saved details are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from that password (PBKDF2, 310,000 iterations). The unlocked key is kept only in memory and is cleared when Chrome closes. The password itself is never stored.

When your data is used

Only when you click Fill this page or press the keyboard shortcut. The extension then writes your saved details into the booking form on the page you are viewing, and shows a small card on that page saying what was filled. By default it does this only on TTD's booking sites (ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in and tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.in); you can allow other sites in Settings. It never presses Continue, Submit or Pay, never solves a CAPTCHA, and never acts on a page without your action. Whatever you submit to TTD is covered by TTD's own policies.

Photos or documents you attach for Srivari Seva are sent into the page only when the booking type is Srivari Seva or Group Seva, and only to fill that page's upload box.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, credit checks, or any purpose other than filling the forms you choose.

Backups

If you use Export, Chrome saves a backup file on your computer, normally in your Downloads folder (or wherever Chrome asks you to save it, if you have turned that on). If a master password is set, the backup stays encrypted. Without one, the file is not encrypted and anyone who has it can read the ID numbers in it; the extension warns you about this before exporting. Keep the file somewhere private.

Deleting your data

Delete individual pilgrims in the extension, use Reset everything on the lock screen, or remove the extension from Chrome, which deletes all of its stored data.

Permissions

  • storage, unlimitedStorage: to keep your details, including an optional photo or document, on your computer.
  • activeTab, scripting: to fill the booking form in the tab you are on, only when you ask, and to show the result card on that page.
  • alarms, notifications: to remind you before quota releases. A few common releases are switched on at install with reminders 1 day and 15 minutes before; you can change or switch them off in the Releases tab.

Children

The extension is meant to be used by adults booking for themselves and their family, including children travelling with them.
